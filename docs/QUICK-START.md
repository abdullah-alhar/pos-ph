# VetPOS: first day at the shop

## Install

1. Run `VetPOS.App-win-Setup.exe`. If Windows asks, choose **More info → Run anyway**.
2. Type the shop's name, your name, a username and a 4 to 8 digit PIN.
3. To practise first, tick **Add 24 sample products**. You can switch them off later in Products.

## Sign in

Click your face, then type your PIN on the keyboard or the round keypad and press Enter.
Ctrl+L locks the screen when you step away; the open bill stays.

## Sell

| Do this | How |
|---|---|
| Add an item | Scan the barcode, or type part of its name, then use ↑ ↓ and Enter |
| More or fewer | + and − on the line, or F3 to type the quantity |
| Change the price on this bill | Click the price, or F4 (the shelf price does not change) |
| Discount on one item | F5: Rs off each, Rs off the line, or % |
| Discount on the whole bill | F6, or in the payment box (Rs / % switch) |
| Take payment | F12. Cash: type what the customer gave you, or click a note. Card or bank: the exact amount is already filled in, so just press Enter |
| Part by card, the rest in cash | Press K, type the card amount, press Enter, then type the cash |
| Hold a bill / bring it back | F8 / F9 |
| Print the last bill again | F10 |
| Find an old bill, print it again or change it | F7 |

## Find sales and bills

* Click the VetPOS logo to return to Home, or click **Search** to find a product, bill, or screen.
* To check what sold today, open **Reports → Today → Find product sales**. Type part of the name or
  scan the barcode and press Enter. Select **All products** to return to the shop totals.
* For other dates, open **Reports → Sales** and choose a day, week, month, year, or your own dates.

## Stock and products

* **Stock in:** a new delivery. Supplier and invoice number are optional.
* **Products:** add products one at a time, or many at once with **Add many with AI** (opens the AI
  chat: describe or paste them, check the card, press Confirm). Each pack size is its own product,
  for example "Whiskas Adult 1.2 kg". **Remove** (owner) deletes an unused product or takes a used
  one off sale.
* **Labels:** print price and barcode stickers.

## AI helper (Ctrl+J)

* Ask: "How much did we sell today?", "What is running low?", "Profit this month?"
* Owner can also ask it to make changes: "create kitten food 1kg, 3kg, 5kg from 1899, each 2000 more",
  "change Pedigree 10kg price to 6400", "we got 10 bags of Pedigree 10kg". It shows a card;
  **nothing is saved until you press Confirm**.
* Click **Speak**, say your question, then review the words and press **Ask**. **Read answer** speaks
  the latest reply. Voice uses the speech language, microphone, and voice installed in Windows.
* To set it up, open **Settings → AI**, choose a provider and paste its key. Click **Test AI**,
  then **Save** once it passes. Leave **Model** empty to use the recommended one.

## Your shop's look

Settings → Shop → **logo** (shows on receipts, sign-in, the desktop icon and the loading screen).
Settings → Appearance → **colour**.

## Every day

* Backups are made automatically every day. Settings → Backups → **Backup now** makes one at any time.
* New versions download by themselves. When "VetPOS x.y is ready" shows at the bottom left,
  click **Restart now** when the shop is quiet, or leave it to install the next time VetPOS closes.

## Something went wrong?

* Messages tell you what happened and what to do. Your bills are safe.
* If a message has a reference such as `E-7K2Q`, write it down.
* Settings → About → **Save a support file** puts a zip on the Desktop. Send it to support
  with the reference and what you were doing. It contains no bills and no PINs.
